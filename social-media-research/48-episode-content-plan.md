# Long-Term Content Plan — AI for Spreadsheet, Google Docs, Google Slides

**Audience:** pemula yang ingin belajar AI untuk kerja dan produktivitas
**Goal:** edukasi + jangkauan melalui TikTok/Instagram Reels
**Series length:** 48 episode per material = **192 episode total**
**Research window:** July 6–October 4, 2026; Instagram fallback digunakan ketika TikTok tidak stabil

## Series operating system

- **Episode label:** `Sheets 01/48`, `Docs 01/48`, `Slides 01/48`, `AI 01/48` supaya penonton tahu ini seri jangka panjang.
- **Structure 30–60 detik:** 0–2s hook/result → 2–6s problem → 6–40s screen demo → 40–52s proof/limitation → 52–60s CTA.
- **Recreating rule:** setiap tutorial harus punya input nyata, output terlihat, langkah yang bisa diikuti, dan batasan yang jujur.
- **Research-to-content rule:** gunakan pola yang ditemukan di `instagram-research.md`, tetapi jangan menyalin script/caption akun lain.
- **Measurement:** hook retention, completion rate, saves, shares, comments, profile visits, dan episode-to-episode follow-through.
- **Safety:** anonimisasi data, jangan tampilkan token/API key, review AI output, dan verifikasi UI/feature availability sebelum merekam.

## Series architecture

- **Episodes 01–12:** foundation and quick wins.
- **Episodes 13–24:** AI-assisted workflows and visible transformations.
- **Episodes 25–36:** Apps Script, integrations, and repeatable automation.
- **Episodes 37–48:** practical systems, QA, governance, and capstone.

## Recommended learning order

1. Sheets foundation → AI assistance → Apps Script automation → practical systems.
2. Docs foundation → AI writing/research → document automation → knowledge systems.
3. Slides foundation → AI storytelling → Apps Script production → presentation systems.
4. AI foundation → Workspace use cases → automation/agents → capstone workflow.
