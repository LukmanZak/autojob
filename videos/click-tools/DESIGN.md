# Click Tools Motion Design

## Style Prompt
Create a cinematic, premium promo for a practical tools account. Keep the canvas light and clean, with warm editorial materials, confident dark typography, restrained champagne accents, generous negative space, and deliberate camera-like movement. Make the motion feel composed and useful rather than flashy; use abstract vector interface motifs, never fabricated product screenshots.

## Colors
- Warm porcelain canvas: `#F4F1EA`
- Deep ink typography: `#1F252B`
- Muted graphite support: `#687078`
- Champagne accent: `#B8895B`
- Soft stone surfaces: `#E6E2D9`

## Typography
- Display and body: Montserrat, 500–900
- Small technical labels: IBM Plex Mono, 400–700

## What NOT to Do
- No neon, cyberpunk palette, dark full-screen scene, or loud color cycling.
- No excessive bounce, glitch cuts, particles, or generic equalizer effects.
- No unsupported product features, performance claims, fake metrics, URL, or account handle.
- No fabricated product screenshot; all interface-like art is explicitly abstract.
- No small text below 24px in the rendered frame.
- No added narration or music.
