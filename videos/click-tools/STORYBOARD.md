# Click Tools — 10-Second Storyboard

**Story:** A short, refined category montage introduces the range of Click Tools, then resolves into a clear follow CTA. The style is cinematic and premium on a light, clean canvas; no voiceover, music, logo asset, URL, or handle is assumed.

| Time | Beat | On-screen copy | Visual experience |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.00–1.20 | Brand opener | `CLICK TOOLS` · `Tools for the way you work.` | A precise cursor glides into a warm porcelain field and clicks once. A fine champagne ripple expands as the typographic wordmark resolves from soft focus. |
| 1.20–3.00 | Automation | `Automate the busywork.` | Three abstract task tiles assemble in sequence; a connector draws through them to suggest a workflow without depicting a real product UI. |
| 3.00–4.60 | Everyday AI | `AI for everyday life.` | A simple abstract assistant panel and idea chips form from the same tile language; nothing reads as a real generated answer. |
| 4.60–6.30 | Business & enterprise | `Built for business & enterprise.` | The tiles expand into an orderly, calm system of connected panels, suggesting breadth without invented metrics. |
| 6.30–7.90 | AI visuals | `Create visuals with AI.` | A tasteful mosaic of abstract, warm-toned image cards reveals; no third-party images or fabricated screenshots are used. |
| 7.90–10.00 | End card | `CLICK TOOLS` · `Automation. AI. Creativity.` · `Follow for smarter tools.` | The panels settle into a spacious wordmark lockup. The CTA holds legibly through the final frame. |

**Rhythm:** click–resolve / assemble–hold / assemble–hold / expand–hold / reveal–hold / slow settle–CTA hold. Keep transitions soft and consistent; let the final card breathe.
